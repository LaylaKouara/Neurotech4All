Dr Arvaneh Speaks at Ulster University ISRC-CN3 Summer School

ARIA Neurotech Inclusion Events Talks 28 Aug 2025 · M A Mohamed · 3 min read

At the ISRC-CN3 Summer School hosted by Ulster University, Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh delivered two talks on implicit brain-computer interaction and the roadmap towards equitable neurotechnology.

Dr Arvaneh at ISRC-CN3 Summer School

On 28 August 2025, Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh was invited to speak at the ISRC-CN3 Summer School, hosted by Ulster University.

Dr Arvaneh delivered two talks: 1. Implicit Brain-Computer Interaction 2. Roadmap Towards Equitable Neurotechnology

These talks highlighted both the technical foundations of implicit interaction paradigms in brain-computer interfaces and the broader vision for equity and inclusion in neurotechnology design.

Her contributions formed part of an international programme bringing together leading researchers, students, and practitioners to discuss cutting-edge advances in neural interfaces and cognitive technologies.

More details about the event can be found on Ulster University’s official website: ISRC-CN3 Summer School
