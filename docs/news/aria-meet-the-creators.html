Our Project Featured on ARIA's 'Meet the Creators'

ARIA Neurotech Inclusion Research Media 15 Oct 2025 · M A Mohamed · 4 min read

Our project is highlighted on ARIA's official website as part of the Future Adoption Opportunity Space, showcasing our work on equitable and inclusive neurotechnology.

Featured on ARIA’s “Meet the Creators”

We are proud to share that our project has been officially featured on ARIA’s website as part of the Future Adoption Opportunity Space under the Precision Neurotechnologies programme.

The profile, titled “Addressing Inequities and Adoption Disparities in Neurotechnology Among Marginalised Groups”, highlights our mission to promote the equitable development and adoption of neurotechnologies.

About the Project

Led by Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh at the University of Sheffield, the project focuses on:

  • Addressing barriers and disparities in neurotechnology design, performance, and adoption among marginalised groups.
  • Using community-based participatory methods and patient-centred research to evaluate biases across diverse demographics.
  • Providing practical recommendations to researchers, clinicians, policymakers, and regulators for more responsible innovation.

The vision is to ensure that future neurotechnologies deliver improved performance, equitable access, and responsible adoption.

Watch the Video

ARIA has also published a short video as part of the profile, where Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh explains the project’s goals and impact.

Why This Matters

Being featured on ARIA’s platform is an important milestone for our team. It signals national recognition of the need to make neurotechnology more inclusive, trustworthy, and accessible.

This opportunity also allows us to share our approach with a wider audience of researchers, innovators, and policymakers, ensuring that equity remains at the forefront of neurotechnology development.

You can view our full profile on ARIA’s website here: Meet the Creators - Future Adoption
