Inclusive Neurotechnology at BrainTech Alliance London

ARIA Neurotech Inclusion Events Talks 9 Sep 2025 · M A Mohamed · 2 min read

Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh gave a talk on inclusive neurotechnology at the BrainTech Alliance event in London on Tuesday, 9 September 2025.

Upcoming Talk in London

On Tuesday, 9 September 2025, Dr Mahnaz Arvaneh delivered a talk on inclusive neurotechnology at the BrainTech Alliance event in London.

This event brought together experts, innovators, and stakeholders to explore advances in neurotechnology and the importance of inclusivity in design, testing, and adoption.

Her presentation highlighted how ensuring equity in neurotechnology development can reduce bias, widen accessibility, and shape more responsible innovations.
